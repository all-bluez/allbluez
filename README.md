# AllBlueZ

Indie apps, made in India 🇮🇳, simple and beautiful, built to help you do what matters.

## Our apps
- **WakeGoal**: the alarm that wakes you up for your goal. Modes for study, gym and work,
  goal countdowns, and a math mission so you actually get out of bed. *(Android)*
- **Fragmio**: fast, simple clip editing for Windows.
